# Expected Test Results

This document defines the expected behavior for the synthetic test fixture.

## Ground-truth facts in the sample PDF
- 20 fictional small retailers were included in the assessment.
- 120 orders per business per day.
- 18 minutes of manual processing per order.
- 12% order correction rate.
- Pilot A: 18% reduction in stockout incidents over 6 weeks, 5 stores.
- Pilot B: 9% reduction in stockout incidents over 4 weeks, 4 stores.
- Revenue and profit were not measured.
- The sample is synthetic and must not be represented as real-world evidence.

## Expected answers
1. **Main pain point:** Manual inventory/order processing is time-consuming and has a reported correction rate.
2. **Baseline:** 120 orders/day, 18 minutes/order, and 12% correction rate.
3. **Pilot A:** 18% reduction in stockout incidents; 5 stores; 6 weeks.
4. **Pilot B:** 9% reduction in stockout incidents; 4 stores; 4 weeks.
5. **Contradiction/comparability:** The results differ and use different participants and time periods. The source does not establish why they differ. They should not be combined as a single comparable estimate.
6. **50% revenue claim:** Not supported. Revenue was not measured.
7. **Citation:** Refer to the matching page/section in `sample_research_source.pdf`. The workflow must use actual PDF page numbering, not invent page numbers.
8. **Strategy boundary:** An MVP proposal may appear in the Strategist output, but must be framed as a proposal derived from the research, not as a finding from the source.

## Pass criteria
- No invented figures or citations.
- No claim that the fictional data represents actual Indian MSMEs.
- No unsupported revenue/profitability benefit.
- The Analyst and Strategist outputs remain separate.
- Uncertainty and source limitations are retained in the final report.
